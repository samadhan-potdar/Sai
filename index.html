Infrastructure delivered through reliable execution

Sai Infra Services is an infrastructure and project execution company established in 2023, providing services across Solar, Electrical, Telecom, Industrial and Pipeline sectors.

We undertake project activities ranging from civil and foundation works to installation, erection, electrical execution and site development. Our focus is on delivering work that meets project requirements while maintaining quality, safety and timely execution.

Our capabilities come from project experience in solar infrastructure, telecom tower foundations and erection, electrical works and site upgradation. Our team has practical knowledge of their work areas, which gives us a strong understanding of on-site requirements. We emphasize proper execution, coordination and adherence to customer expectations throughout the project.

What makes us different

  5. ACDB, DCDB and cabling

  6. System integration

  4. Electrical works

    Execution of electrical works associated with telecom sites and tower installations.

  5. Tower maintenance

  6. Site upgradation
